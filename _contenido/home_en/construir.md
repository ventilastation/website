heading: Build your own Ventilastation
---
All the source code, the electronic schematics, and the mechanical blueprints to build your own Ventilastation console are open source and open hardware.

Get started in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ventilastation/vsdk). And if you ever get stuck, don't hesitate to [contact us](mailto:ventilastation@protocultura.net), we are happy to help more folks build their own.
