Ventilastation is an open source electromechanical game console with an odd display, built for creating and playing circular video games.\\
Fully designed, published and built in Argentina.

The evolution of [Super Ventilagon](https://ventilagon.protocultura.net/) (2015), it builds on its predecessor's circular display by adding sprite based 8-bit like graphics, and super easy programmability with Micropython.
